What we are trying to do here

See where people do their best work, where AI can help them, and where routine work can come off their plate.

Our guiding principles

  1. Support, not replaceAI supports HR judgment and human connection. It never replaces them.
  2. A named ownerA person is accountable for every process and every AI-assisted decision.
  3. Human reviewHigh-impact decisions about hiring, pay, performance and exit should not be left to AI alone. Take legal advice first.
  4. Fair, private, honestUse only the data needed, check for bias, and say plainly where AI is used and its limits.
  5. Pilot small, then adaptHours and labels are starting guesses to test, not final answers.

Our order of thinking

  1. People firstPeople affected want fairness and clear updates. HR teams and managers bring judgment, warmth and trust. These stay with people.
  2. Then AI helpsAI helps a person prepare, so they decide better.
  3. Last, routineA workflow handles routine tasks. A person checks the results.

Every task gets one of four labels

Human-ledPeople do it. AI helps at most in the background.
AugmentAI prepares, a person decides.
AutomateA workflow does it, a person spot-checks.
CreateNew things AI makes possible.

About this pagewhat you get, who it helps

What you get

Labels, weights and hours give a working hypothesis for each process: the share of tasks, and the time, under each label.

Who it helps

People in HR, hiring managers and the employees affected. Also any team moving towards AI that wants to look at its work one task at a time.

What it is not

The hours and labels are starting guesses to test. AI supports people, and a person stays accountable.
